Entry Conditions:
• Directional Confidence: >= 70%
• IV Rank: < 50 (low volatility environment)
• IV Skew: Front month IV > Back month IV
• Liquidity: Bid-ask spread < 0.05

Position Structure:
• Long Leg: 45 DTE, Delta 0.75 (Deep ITM)
• Short Leg: 10 DTE, Delta 0.30 (OTM)
• Max Debit: <= 75% of strike width

Rolling Strategy:
• Standard Roll: At 7 DTE or 50% profit on short leg
• Adjustment Roll: When delta breaches target range ±0.15
• Goal: Collect premium 3-6 times per long leg

Exit Rules:
• Profit: 50% of max profit OR 4+ successful rolls
• Loss: Down 100% (full debit) OR direction reversal
• Time: Long leg < 21 DTE and unprofitable
